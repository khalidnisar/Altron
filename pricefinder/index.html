How Altron PriceFinder works

1 · Search. Type a product, hotel, route or city — or upload a photo. PriceFinder queries trusted retailers and travel metasearch engines across the web.

2 · Compare. Every quote is normalised into your chosen currency and ranked lowest → highest. The bar at the top shows the full price spread; green = lowest, red = highest.

3 · Get the edge. 🕶️ Incognito strips cookies & personalization so retailers can’t price-markup your history. 📍 Geo-spoofing re-quotes prices from another country (flights & hotels often differ by origin). 🏷️ Coupons are auto-searched and only *verified, unexpired* codes are shown.

4 · Buy. Hit Buy on any quote — it opens the real retailer page with your search pre-filled.

Only merchants with real footfall and reputation are shown by default; spammy zero-traffic sites are filtered out automatically. Exchange rates come from live public FX feeds with a static fallback.
